## SEO and website optimisation in Perth

SEO for a Perth service business is about owning the right intents — service, industry and local — without publishing thin suburb swaps or stuffing keywords into pages nobody would send a client.

We align page purpose, technical quality and internal links so search traffic can convert. Rankings are an outcome of clarity and usefulness, not a promise we sell. We do not guarantee positions.

## What our SEO work covers

Technical health: indexation, crawl issues, performance basics and Core Web Vitals where they block results. On-page structure: titles, headings, intent match and thin-content cleanup. Information architecture that supports clusters (services, industries, locations) without doorway pages. Content planning or writing for pages that deserve to rank. Measurement: search visibility for agreed terms, organic enquiry quality and issues closed — in plain language.

Work is sequenced. If Search Console shows the site is barely indexed, writing more blog posts is the wrong first spend. If the site is indexed but every service is one vague paragraph, content and IA come first. If people find you and bounce because the page is confusing, that is a [redesign](/services/website-redesign/) conversation, not more meta tags.

## When SEO is not the first job

SEO is a poor fit when the website cannot take an enquiry, the offer is about to change, or you want “number one for a generic term” with no page that deserves it. It is also the wrong retainer if the real leak is paid campaigns with no [landing page](/services/landing-page-design/) — organic work will not rescue a mismatched ad.

[Tradies](/industries/tradies-construction/) often need service-area honesty more than fifty suburb URLs; see [what makes a good tradie website](/blog/website-for-tradies-guide/). [Professional services](/industries/professional-services/) need pages that explain matters and sectors without compliance-sensitive puff. [Real estate](/industries/real-estate/) sites need listing and suburb intent handled carefully so you are not competing with portals on terms you cannot win.

## Local SEO without spam

Where Google Business Profile and local intent matter, we work on consistency and genuine service-area clarity. We do not manufacture a unique page for every suburb with the same paragraph and a postcode change. Link building, when relevant, focuses on earnable mentions — not bought spam packages.

NAP consistency (name, address, phone) across the site, profile and directories still matters. Categories, photos and review replies are operational work you must own; we can advise, we cannot pretend to be your front desk.

## Decisions, integrations and risk

You will decide which services are worth a dedicated page, which locations are real coverage versus wishful, who approves factual claims, and whether we implement changes or your developers do. Access to Search Console, analytics, CMS and the Business Profile is not optional if you want diagnosis rather than guesswork.

Risks: a previous SEO provider’s doorway network that needs cleanup; a [migration](/services/website-migration/) that already dropped equity; duplicate title tags across a CMS; and blogging for its own sake. Recovering from spammy tactics can mean removing pages, not adding them. We will say when a [custom rebuild](/services/custom-website-design/) or IA change is a prerequisite.

Integrations are mostly measurement and publishing: Search Console, analytics with enquiry events, CMS fields for titles and canonicals, and sometimes a CRM so organic leads are tagged. We do not need a dozen SEO plugins. One well-configured setup beats three that fight.

## How we measure, report and hand over

We report search visibility for an agreed term set, technical issues closed, pages published or improved, and organic enquiry quality where tracking exists. Impressions without leads are not a success story. Technical fixes can help quickly; content and authority take months — we set expectations against your starting point, not a fantasy calendar.

Handover of an SEO engagement includes the roadmap, what was changed, and how to keep internal links and titles sensible as you add pages. Ongoing CMS and plugin updates belong on [maintenance](/services/website-maintenance/). Campaign conversion pages remain a [landing page](/services/landing-page-design/) job. How we sequence work sits on [our process](/process/) page.

## SEO vs redesign vs landing pages

SEO improves findability of what you have (or are building). [Redesign](/services/website-redesign/) changes experience. [Landing pages](/services/landing-page-design/) convert campaigns. They often work together; they are not interchangeable labels for “make us rank #1”.
