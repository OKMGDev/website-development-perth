Website cost in Perth varies because projects vary. A focused brochure site is a different job from a multi-location service platform or a commerce catalogue.

## What usually drives the price

- Number of unique templates and integrations
- Content writing and asset preparation
- Migrations, redirects and SEO protection
- Accessibility, performance and CMS training needs

## Ballpark thinking (not a quote)

Simple marketing sites often land in a lower band when content is ready. Custom design systems, e-commerce and applications sit higher because the risk and engineering surface grow.
