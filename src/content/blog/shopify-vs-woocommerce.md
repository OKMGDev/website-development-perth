Shopify and WooCommerce can both sell products well. The better choice depends on who will run the store day to day.

## Choose Shopify when

You want a hosted platform, predictable commerce primitives and less server maintenance.

## Choose WooCommerce when

You already run WordPress, need unusual catalogue or membership logic, or want store and content deeply intertwined.
